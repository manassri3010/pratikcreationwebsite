Studio Suzao is a limited edition clothing studio in Shanghai's Xuhui district, built on a philosophy of handcrafted garments. Their Chinese tagline — 以布为语，以针为笔 — translates as "fabric as words, needles as pen." The brief was simple: the print had to hold the same seriousness as the clothes.

We produced a two-sided business card suite on 600gsm uncoated stock. Front face: white ground, a half-field orange block, the brand mark set in bold alongside stacked Chinese characters. Reverse: solid orange, contact details centred, no decoration. Finished in soft-touch matte laminate across both faces.

The orange is unapologetic. The typography is considered. At this weight of card, everything either earns its presence or it doesn't.
