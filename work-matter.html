A full packaging suite
built for a considered brand.

MATTER came to us with a clear brief: sustainable materials, premium finish, nothing excessive. A London-based fashion label launching their first full collection — they needed packaging that felt as considered as the clothes inside it.

We built the entire suite in-house across three weeks: a rigid shirt box with gold foil stamp on a soft-touch matte lid, kraft paper bags with rope handle and blind debossed monogram, woven labels in high-density damask, and die-cut hang tags with spot UV finish.

Every finishing process — foil, deboss, UV — was completed in our Mumbai facility. The full order shipped to London by air in 12 days from production sign-off.
