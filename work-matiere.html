MATIÈRE's No. 07 is a Vétiver eau de parfum produced in editions of 200 units, each bottle serial-numbered at the base. The formula was developed across sixteen reformulations under the direction of nose Hélène Caron in Grasse. A product at this level of specificity demands print that can carry the same weight.

We produced the accompanying brand document — a two-panel insert: a black-field left page carrying only the edition number, and an uncoated sand-tone right page with full formula disclosure and provenance notes printed in a single weight of type.

170gsm uncoated stock. No laminate. No finishing. The restraint is deliberate. At this price point, decoration is a signal of insecurity. The quality of the paper and the precision of the print do all the work.
