Seventeen
years of craft.

Pratik Creation was founded in Mumbai in 2007 — a small operation with a single conviction: that garment packaging in India didn't have to look the way it did. Cheap, inconsistent, an afterthought.

Seventeen years later, we manufacture premium packaging for fashion and fragrance brands across the UK, UAE, and India. Every finish — foil, emboss, UV — completed in-house, in Mumbai, to the same standard we held ourselves to on day one.

What changed is the scale. What didn't is the insistence on quality.

How it started
2007
A gap in the market. And a decision to fill it.

When Pratik Creation opened in Mumbai, most garment packaging came from one of two places: volume domestic suppliers who prioritised throughput over quality, or expensive international manufacturers out of reach for mid-size brands. There was nothing credible in between. We started as that something in between — a small factory that cared about the finish, the material, and what the box felt like in someone's hands.

2012
Building the capability, not the client list.

For the first decade, we didn't focus on growing the number of clients. We focused on growing what we could do. Each year brought a new machine, a new finish, a new process brought in-house. Foil stamping. Embossing. UV coating. Soft-touch laminate. By 2015 we had every major packaging finish under one roof — something almost no manufacturer at our scale could match. The clients followed the capability.

2019
Our clients started going global. So did we.

Then came a shift. The Indian brands we'd built with started exporting — to the UK, to the Middle East, to Europe. Their packaging now needed to meet a different standard. The standard Western buyers and luxury fragrance houses expect. We realised that what we'd built in Mumbai — quality, consistency, in-house finishing — was exactly what international brands needed and couldn't easily source. We stopped being a local supplier. We became a global manufacturer.

Now
Mumbai manufacturing. Global clients.

Today, Pratik Creation serves fashion and fragrance brands across the UK, UAE, China, and India. The factory is in Mumbai — the same building, the same hands, the same obsession with finish quality. The difference is dedicated international client management across every market we serve. Same-day responses. Transparent lead times. Production photo updates at every stage. Sample kits dispatched fast — wherever you are.

How we work
01
Nothing leaves without your approval.

Every order starts with a physical pre-production sample. You sign off on it in writing before a single machine runs the full batch. Dimensions, colour, finish — confirmed before we begin. No surprises when the box arrives.

02
Finishing stays in the building.

Foil stamping, embossing, UV coating, soft-touch laminate — we do all of it ourselves. Not because we have to. Because quality control disappears the moment a job leaves your factory. Ours doesn't leave.

03
We work to your timeline.

Dedicated client management across every market we serve — same-day responses, transparent lead times, and production photo updates at every stage. You're never left wondering where your order is.
